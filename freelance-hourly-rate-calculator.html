How this works

Most freelancers only bill 40–60% of the hours they work, because admin, sales and gaps are unpaid. So the rate must be based on billable hours. The formula: (take-home ÷ (1 − tax rate) + expenses) × (1 + buffer) ÷ billable hours.

Treat the result as your floor, not your price: price on the value you deliver. Then put your rate on a professional invoice with a clear scope.

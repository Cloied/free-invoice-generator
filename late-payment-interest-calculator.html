Charging late fees properly

Simple interest = amount × annual rate × days ÷ 365. A late fee or interest is only enforceable if it is stated in your contract or invoice terms and allowed where you operate: many countries set statutory rates for business-to-business debts, so check yours.

Tip: write “Late payments incur X% annual interest” in the invoice notes before you send it, and send a polite reminder at 7 days overdue.
